Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 13, 2025

1. No Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information. When you enter a username to join the chat, it is used only temporarily in your browser. It is not stored on our servers.

2. Temporary Message Storage

Messages sent in the chat are temporarily stored for 10 minutes so that users can view recent conversations. After that, they are automatically deleted. No personal data is associated with the messages.

3. Cookies

We do not use cookies or any form of tracking.

4. Third Parties

We do not share data with third parties because we don’t collect any.

5. Security

Since we do not permanently store data, there is no risk of long-term data misuse through this website.

6. Contact

If you have any questions, you can contact us at: info@unlike.gr or by using our Contact Form
